Frequently asked questions

What is a TFC credit?

TFC (TokenForge Credit) is our internal currency. 1 TFC = $1 USD. You buy credits upfront and they're deducted automatically as you make API calls. No monthly fees, no subscriptions.

What's the minimum purchase?

The minimum purchase is $10 (10 TFC). Credits never expire.

How do I switch between models?

Just change the model parameter in your request. No SDK changes, no new API keys needed.

Is TokenForge more expensive than going direct?

TokenForge adds a 20% platform fee over provider costs. In exchange you get one API key, one dashboard, one bill, project tracking, and balance alerts — instead of managing 3+ accounts.

What happens when my credits run out?

Requests are automatically blocked — you'll never be charged more than your balance. You'll receive email alerts at 20% and 10% remaining.
